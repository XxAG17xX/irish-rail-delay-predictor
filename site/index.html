Irish Rail delays,
as a range.

Station boards tell you a train is expected at 17:09. They don't tell you how sure they are. RailCast answers most likely 17:09, probably between 17:07 and 17:14 — and then publishes how often that range was right.

26% closer than the operator's own estimate on matched predictions, scored nightly
80% is what the range claims measured every night against what happened
5 min between updates predictions are logged before the train arrives

The honest limitation, up front: the ranges are calibrated for ordinary lateness. On a real disruption — a train losing an hour in one stretch — they miss every time, and the accuracy page says so with the number attached.
