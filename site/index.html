Every answer is checked

  1. Written down first

    Each prediction is logged with its time, train, station and range before the outcome exists. The service that writes predictions cannot read them back.

  2. Scored the next morning

    At 06:15 a job fetches what actually happened and marks every prediction inside or outside its range. It can read the log but never write to it.

  3. Published as it falls

    The record goes on the accuracy page by line, beside the 80% claimed, with the date it covers and the model that made it.
