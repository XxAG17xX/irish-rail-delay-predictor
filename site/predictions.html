Why some services have no range

The model reads how late a train already is at the stops behind you. A service that has not left its origin has reported nowhere, so there is nothing to reason from. That is most of what you see at a terminus like Heuston, where nearly everything on the board is a departure, and much less at a through station like Kildare.

This is the coverage limitation from the accuracy page, in the place a passenger actually meets it. Listing those services and marking them is deliberate: dropping them would make the coverage figure look better than the thing you are standing in front of.
